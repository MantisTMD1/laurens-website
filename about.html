From teaching high school to personal training

I grew up in Waukesha and studied at Carroll University, where I found a passion for coaching and teaching. I paired it with my interest in science and the human body, and graduated with high honors.

I taught fitness and health education at Sussex Hamilton High School. I wanted a gym class where students found ways of being active they enjoyed, so exercise never felt like a chore or a punishment.

In 2018 I became a certified personal trainer so I could help more people than my students. I started at a bootcamp with large groups, and have grown to love giving direct, individual coaching in a smaller setting.

My approach to training

“Give a man a fish and you feed him for a day; teach a man to fish and you feed him for a lifetime.” I coach the same way: you leave knowing how to train, not only what to do today.

Move safely, and enjoy it

I teach you how to move safely, find activity you enjoy, and keep balance and moderation in a healthy lifestyle.

Hours in the gym are not the goal

Training should let you do the things you love, lower your stress and challenge your mind and body, without living at the gym.

Find your why

Trust lets me write a plan for you alone. You find your reason to train and own a routine of movement you enjoy, manageable nutrition and more confidence.
